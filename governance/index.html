VeriTech Group Data Governance Programme

Programme design · Keithan Cokbilir

The gap is never knowledge alone. It's always environment, confidence, and a reason to act.

Brief. VeriTech Group, a fictional standards-driven organisation, is rolling out data governance. Three new roles — Data Owner, Data Steward and Use Case Owner — must start making decisions that used to go to IT, but nobody is clear who decides what.

Most data literacy programmes deliver content and measure completion. I designed for the moments that matter instead: the first governance meeting, the first real business question in Power BI, the first decision that no longer goes to IT.

My role: needs analysis, learning journey architecture, simulation and activity design, facilitator materials, manager alignment strategy, measurement framework.
StatusConcept project — fully designed, not yet delivered. All artefacts below are working and open offline in a new tab.
Key design decisions
  1. Journeys built around day-one tasksEach role's journey starts from what that person has to do in their first week, not from a governance curriculum.
  2. An 8-minute card sort replaces a cold startLearners assign 12 real scenarios to a role before session 1, so the session opens with genuine disagreement to work through.
  3. Information asymmetry in the simulationEach role gets a different brief, so the cross-role scenario can only be solved by negotiating — the same skill the roles need on the job.

Measurement, feedback and contingency

How we know the programme worked — a complete measurement arc from day zero to day 30, the mechanism that closes the Voice your blocks feedback loop, and a designed response for when participation falls below threshold.
Open measurement framework

If they don't use it — I've failed.

The design is not measured by completion rates. It is meant to produce Data Owners who make decisions, Data Stewards who resolve conflicts, and Use Case Owners who submit briefs that technical teams can act on. If those behaviours don't change at day 30, the design goes back to the action map.
